What this app enforces on every row, and what it deliberately leaves to you.
Where a rule and a rating disagree, the audit rail names the SC or Convention and the fix.



Action triggers — Convention #5




CustomerRecommended Action is required when


L3Harris — all partsRPN ≥ 100, regardless of Sev


Default — other customers(RPN ≥ 100 and Sev ≥ 7) or RPN ≥ 200




House amendment #5A (2026-07-28).
Rows below the trigger carry no Recommended Action at all — column T stays empty at RPN < 100 —
with two exceptions that keep their Best Practice text because other rules depend on it:
Mitsubishi per-part-dressing rows (the Occ credit is conditional on the formalization action, §3)
and CNC program-edit rows (the lockdown reference stays on every machine so the action chain is consistent, §3).
The audit flags any other sub-100 action; FIX SAFE clears them in one press.


Sev ≥ 9 alone does not trigger.
A Sev 9 row with RPN < 100 stays at Best Practice framing — and is still CC class per SC-32,
because Class tracks the Sev tier independently of trigger state. That combination is correct, not contradictory.
Clause (a) of the old default trigger was removed 2026-05-30.

Detection basisBest Det

Current-state Det reflects the without-R&R ceiling until R&R is documented on that specific gauge.
Action-result projections may commit R&R with a target date.
Project-wide classifications: slug squareness gage is variable (cap 4); go/no-go Deltronic pins on bores
under .0005″ total tolerance are attribute (cap 5); all Mitsubishi grind inspection is 100%, so run-out is Det 4, not 5.



The other five conventions


#1 — Detection is the single strongest control
Redundant controls do not reduce Det. Controls checking different characteristics get their own lines.
Anything in column L or O documents redundancy for audit and earns no rating credit.


#2 — Severity cannot be reduced by inspection
Only a design change, process change, or redefined failure-effect scope moves Sev.
100% inspection changes whether the failure escapes, not the consequence when it does.


#4 — Probing earns Occurrence credit only with auto-offset
No CAMCO machine qualifies today. Hardinge Quest lathes have no probes at all; the Haas UMC has the kit
installed but does not use the auto-offset option. Probing on either is Detection only.


#6 — Residual risk acceptance
When RPN cannot go lower, document the floor and why, that prevention and detection are at AIAG limits or
process capability, and QE plus customer SQE sign-off with a date. Stuck-high RPN with no rationale is an audit finding.



Current-state process facts — Section 3


Facts about how CAMCO operates today, not aspirations. A row crediting a control that does not exist is the
most damaging error this project produces, because it survives review and misrepresents risk to the customer.


Certs are not compared to bar tags at receipt
CX718-1 is authored at Rev-00 but not released; planned operational start Q3 2026.
Cert-vs-tag-dependent rows rate Det 10 until the gate is released and operating.


CNC programs are not locked down at any controller
Hardinge Quest, Mitsubishi grinder, and Haas UMC all allow full operator edit access, with no change-control gate
and no checksum or diff at setup. Every PFMEA covering a CNC machining op carries the program-edit row until lockdown operates.


The Mitsubishi grinder dresses the wheel before every part
Real machine-level prevention worth roughly one Occ step — but it is operator practice, not in a controlled procedure.
Taking the credit requires a formalization action, and the prevention text must disclose the gap.
One procedure retires it for every grind op on the machine.


Hardinge specifics
Main spindle TIR ≤ .0001, so main spindle runout is not a valid cause on turning ops — use tool wear, work-hardening,
programming offset, or bar-feed inconsistency. Live-tool spindle runout is valid on cross-hole ops.
No climb milling and no helical toolpaths; straight drilling, axial plunge, and circular interpolation only.


Military application by default
Jungle, desert, maritime environments; thermal and vibration cycling on a reusable unit.
Write "military hardware," never "flight hardware" or "avionics," and never name a program.
Flag ferromagnetic substitution near IMU, magnetometer, or gimbal sensors.

What this app does not do


No image embedding
SC-25 reference photos, SC-29 vendor certificates, and the SC-36 drawing go in during the XML-surgery pass.
Export from here first, embed second — never the reverse. Once a file carries images, openpyxl will silently
destroy them on save (SC-30), so the surgery path is the only safe one from that point on.


Template geometry is your call
Rev 97 describes the IAQG layout (headers row 16, data 17). The canonical CAMCO template dated 5-27-26 puts headers
at row 6. Both export paths work; pick one in the part strip on the Build screen.
